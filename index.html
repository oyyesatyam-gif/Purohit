Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. It prepares a request. The date, availability, final price, and booking must be confirmed directly with the service provider.

Is puja samagri included?

Choose your preference in the form and confirm the exact inclusions and any extra cost before booking.

Can I choose a preferred date?

Yes. Enter your preferred date and time. Availability is not checked automatically in this demo.

Are the listed prices final?

No. They are sample starting prices for layout demonstration. Replace them with your actual prices before publishing.

Are my bookings stored online?

No. This starter version stores records in the current browser only. A shared database and secure admin dashboard need a backend integration.
